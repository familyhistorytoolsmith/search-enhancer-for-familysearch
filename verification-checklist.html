Relative needed: a woman with a dated marriage who appears in censuses both before and after that year — ideally one who married before age 20 (the old age-heuristic would have gotten her wrong).

  • DEATH entries use the married surname

D  ·  Remaining ⏳ collection ids (one click each)

Relatives needed: one born/christened in Scotland 1564–1950, one born in Ireland 1620–1881, one born in the US 1867–1931.
  • Scotland: “Scotland births & baptisms 1564–1950” opens collection 1771030; results are sensible Scottish baptisms and the results page shows the collection filter chip  ✓ verified 2026-08-31
  • Scotland bonus: censuses 1841–1901 offered; 1911 falls back to a generic in-country search (no single FS collection)
  • Ireland: collection 1584963 — sensible results
  • Ireland bonus: only the 1901 and 1911 censuses are offered (the rest were destroyed)
  • US: “US births & christenings 1867–1931” opens collection 1808995 — the chip names the collection (zero results can be legitimate: only ~37k records)
Fail signal: an error page, a chip reading “Other, <id>” instead of the collection name, or the filter silently ignored. (E&W 1473014 + Scotland 1771030: click-verified 2026-08-31; the dead US id 1675234 was caught by this very check and fixed to 1808995.)
